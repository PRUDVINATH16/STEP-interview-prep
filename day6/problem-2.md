# Problem Statement: Generate a Wave Pattern with K Elements

Given a sequence of numbers from `1` to `N` and an integer `K`, generate a wave pattern containing exactly `K` elements.

The pattern alternates between forward and reverse order.

### Example 1

**Input:**
```text
N = 5
K = 25
```

**Output:**
```text
1 2 3 4 5 4 3 2 1 2 3 4 5 4 3 2 1 2 3 4 5 4 3 2 1
```
